# Pip

Pip is company and a guide, never a wall of text.

- Speech is one or two sentences at a time in a bubble over Pip. It rides with him and never dodges about.
- Two kinds: waiting words (solid box, bright edge, F badge) and free words (light, italic, fade on their own). Only the jetty opening, the crafting lesson and "Home base!" wait for F. Free words go early if you already did the thing.
- He is never still in the early game: he potters where he waits, bounces when excited, wanders while he talks on the jetty.
- Leading: he keeps to one side of your path, 3 to 9 tiles ahead, and never circles you. If you don't follow for a while he goes on ahead, off the screen, and comes back to hurry you.
- Reminders: with no progress for ~16 s (a little longer each time) he walks to something that helps and says it a new way, never the same line twice running.
- He talks about what he is standing near (camp marks, the robin, a patch, a stick you need) and keeps lines short.
- No crafting talk before the lesson (two tufts of fluff in hand).

Code: pip.js (behaviour), tutorial.js (every early-game line, in order, plus PIP_LINES for the rest).
