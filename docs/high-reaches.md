# The High Reaches

Above the old summit (peak3, which now has a path north). Built in build 114 as a first pass; much more to come.

## Screens
- **Windy Ledge (hr1):** the east side drops away. Past the rock lip, the valley far below: forest dot by dot, the river winding, haze, clouds drifting under you, and tiny birds rising from the surface, growing as they come. Two mountain mantises (big insects stay on the lower and middle slopes). Crystal insect statues.
- **The Crossing (hr2):** a ridge with sky on both sides. Mountain hawks. The red crystal beetle, waiting on the rock.
- **Above the Clouds (hr3):** the west side is cloud tops rolling away, the valley through the gaps. Worm breathing tubes (they open and close), tunnels of every size, and a worm who talks. First arrival shows the title card: The High Reaches. The way north (the peak) is closed: nothing that went higher came back.

## Creatures
- **Crystal insects:** grand (blue, cut-glass) or tormented (violet, twisted, spiked). Still for centuries; some twitch when you're close.
- **Mountain hawks:** circle high (only their shadow on you), then dive. A dive knocks you back, or sometimes the talons grab: you're carried off and dropped (hurt). Dropped over the edge, you land a screen lower. Strike (F) while carried and it lets go early.
- **Mantises:** stalk, rear up (eyes red), lunge.
- **The worms:** friendly, lonely, no predators but each other; the kingdoms squabble. The oldest are rooted so deep, they say, they touch the fire at the middle of everything. They will teach the ground ability later.

## The red crystal beetle
- Joins you on the Crossing (F), reluctantly. Rides on you, clambering about.
- Near crystal or out in the wind, it gives back a little vigor. When you're low and have food, it feeds you one (every 30 s at most).

## Vigor
- Resting on its own is slower now (55% of before). Food, the fire, and the beetle are the quick way back.

## Coming later
- The ground ability (push up to lift or strike; drop and stretch to trap), taught by the worms; it may deepen marsh fire too.
- More vantage points higher up; the worm kingdoms; the peak.
