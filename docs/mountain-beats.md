# The mountain: the beats (6 Oct, after 230; the stills are docs/parked/mock-beats.js, quest-beats.png)
A script of what the player goes through, screen by screen, each with one feeling. Proposed; nothing here is built.
Each beat: what you see, what you do, the secret, the sound, and which still shows it.

0. MORNING AT CAMP (no still). Pip is back, the garden is up. A stranger went by at first light: the ornithologist,
   net and kettle, puffing uphill, and he dropped a feather by the lean-to. Pip won't come ("the wind up there is
   too much for me") and gives you the feather. That is the whole reason to climb: find out whose it is.
1. LEAVING HOME (still 1). The rise. The view pulls back as you climb; at a ledge partway up the camp is in view at
   the foot of the screen, tiny, smoke rising. Two rabbits, the reeds, the ravine. Secret: nothing but the look back;
   a line from Pip's feather in the wind if you stand there a while. Sound: f1's wind, the gusts.
2. AWE (still 2). m1 the climb. The mountain's face across the whole screen, sheets stacked, the path a thread up them.
   Dawn light: warm from the east on every top, cool in the lees, lichen rust and sage on the sunlit tops. You climb
   shelf by shelf; a hawk circles and never comes. Secret: a nest in a notch, the first egg (the ornithologist's).
   Sound: wind higher and thinner, one bird call you never place.
3. PLAY (still 3). m2 the ledges, the brush level (docs/parked/mock-ledges.js). Close view. Ridges to hop, the
   horseshoe with carrots and two bigger rabbits already squabbling over them, the shelf with the eave, the sinkhole,
   the cave, the crack. Secrets: the stepping stone behind the horseshoe; under the eave the ornithologist's cold
   fire ring and a second feather; the sinkhole's tunnel that comes out behind the horseshoe. Sound: rabbit grumbles,
   the first grit in the wind. The tone lifts here: this screen is a toy.
4. HUSH (still 4). m3 inside the mountain, in through the sinkhole's deep end (the pit into the ground). Dark; the
   window round you is your lamp; the floor wet, drips; no enemies. Dead ends roofed, and in one a mushroom glowing
   violet: the spores' home, the one you can rest and spore-travel from. Once, far off through the stone, Pip's
   voice (the spores carry it): the loneliest and warmest beat. Secret: the mushroom itself; a crack of daylight
   where the roof is missing a slab shows the way on. Sound: drips, your steps, nothing else.
5. STRUGGLE (still 5). m4 the windy crossing, out of the stone into the open. Bare ridges, grit streaming, a wall to
   shelter behind; the gusts in threes, the strong one driving you back toward the cave mouth. Short. Secret: a
   ledge you only reach by riding the strong gust. Sound: the wind at its loudest, then nothing in the lee.
6. RELIEF (still 6). m5 the last ledges in side view, then the tortoise's hollow: the wind drops to a breeze, grass
   again, a peach tree, the ornithologist at his kettle. He knows the feather (his), has the eggs to talk about,
   wants carrot juice; the tortoise wants peaches and gives peach stones and the way on to the crags. Sound: the
   kettle, a breeze, birds. The mountain gives back what the rise took away.

Rules this adds (for design-rules.md once Ross takes it): grey is never the whole palette, every mountain screen keeps
one warm thing in view; every hidden place holds a sign someone was there before you; the view pulls back only for
awe (m1, the top) and comes close for play and hush.
Build order if taken: light on the mountain (still first) > the looking-back ledge > m2 the ledges laid > the pit into
the ground > m3 > m4 > the hollow.
