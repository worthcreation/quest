## Design rules to keep
gusts come in sets of three (two gentle, one strong), only the strong one carries you, landing on a ledge; your shadow shows where you'll land (small at the top of a jump, leading toward the landing). Rocks are buried and must be knocked loose (stomp), then rocked and heaved; single cracking boulders take thrown rocks; no pressure plates, logs or ropes. Rough stones are faceted with a zigzag soil line where they meet the ground; throwing stones are lumpy, each its own shape. Say "rich soil", never "tilled"; the shelter is a lean-to, never a tent. One alert style: small scrolls near the bottom (pickups too); full banners only when a quest starts or ends. The HUD sits bottom-right (vigor bar and the A S D F slots) and fades when quiet; the quest note is top-right. R opens the slot wheel; while R is held, A S D F choose which key to set. F at a patch opens the plant/compost menu. The pack is an icon belt; the Map tab is a page for every place you've been, purple where a found mushroom is; nothing about spores until they answer you. Anything you gain shows in the pack. Pip and NPC lines hold until F; tutorial lines are free; Pip teaches in his own bubble and a slot flashes when Pip suggests using it. Pip is "Pip", never "they"; Pip leads on one side of your path at about half your speed. Items rest on the ground and are picked up with F. Skills level quietly: one flavour line per level, details on the Status page. Quests: current objectives on top, folded log below, newest first.

## Story so far
you choose a name and colour; the game opens on the old jetty mid-conversation; Pip waits by the garden; the robin hides until you pound by its tree, then drops seeds; plant two (quest: Pip's garden); Pip leads to the camp spot (quest: Set up camp; lean-to already up); gather river stones (the last ones are inside a boulder you break with a stuck stone Pip shows you how to loosen), sticks (a few lying about, the rest shaken from trees with a pound) and rabbit fluff; glue, fire ring and workbench on the mat (3 slots); twilight and the lantern in the lean-to; the woods at dusk: a gremlin peeks, another taunts, then an ambush takes Pip; break the boulders; the sword in a stump by a bramble cluster; the cave; the rescue; spores carry you home; morning, and the journal quest. Beyond: the rise (where f2 was: one long slope from f1 that pulls the view back and tips it as you climb, with f1's wind, two rabbits and cover, and a wall of reeds at x 20 that nothing passes yet), the windy fields and mountain path, the crags and the High Reaches above the old summit, spore travel between found mushrooms, Wick's cellar. The climb screens (climb1 to climb5) are in testing and not yet joined to the world.
