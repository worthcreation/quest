## Design rules to keep
gusts come in sets of three (two gentle, one strong), only the strong one carries you, landing on a ledge; your shadow shows where you'll land (small at the top of a jump, leading toward the landing). Rocks are buried and must be knocked loose (stomp), then rocked and heaved; single cracking boulders take thrown rocks; no pressure plates, logs or ropes. Rough stones are faceted with a zigzag soil line where they meet the ground; throwing stones are lumpy, each its own shape. Say "rich soil", never "tilled"; the shelter is a lean-to, never a tent. One alert style: small scrolls near the bottom (pickups too); full banners only when a quest starts or ends. The HUD sits bottom-right (vigor bar and the A S D F slots) and fades when quiet; the quest note is top-right. R opens the slot wheel; while R is held, A S D F choose which key to set. F at a patch opens the plant/compost menu. The pack is an icon belt; the Map tab is a page for every place you've been, purple where a found mushroom is; nothing about spores until they answer you. Anything you gain shows in the pack. Pip and NPC lines hold until F; tutorial lines are free; Pip teaches in his own bubble and a slot flashes when Pip suggests using it. Pip is "Pip", never "they"; Pip leads on one side of your path at about half your speed. Items rest on the ground and are picked up with F. Skills level quietly: one flavour line per level, details on the Status page. Quests: current objectives on top, folded log below, newest first.

## Story so far
you choose a name and colour; the game opens on the old jetty mid-conversation; Pip waits by the garden; the robin hides until you pound by its tree, then drops seeds; plant two (quest: Pip's garden); Pip leads to the camp spot (quest: Set up camp; lean-to already up); gather river stones (the last ones are inside a boulder you break with a stuck stone Pip shows you how to loosen), sticks (a few lying about, the rest shaken from trees with a pound) and rabbit fluff; glue, fire ring and workbench on the mat (3 slots); twilight and the lantern in the lean-to; the woods at dusk: a gremlin peeks, another taunts, then an ambush takes Pip; break the boulders; the sword in a stump by a bramble cluster; the cave; the rescue; spores carry you home; morning, and the journal quest. Beyond: the rise (where f2 was: one long slope from f1 that pulls the view back and tips it as you climb, with f1's wind, two rabbits and cover, and no walls of stone: the screen's edges hold and east of its foot the mountain itself does, except through the pass; a bottomless ravine in from the north edge and a wall of reeds at x 20 together shut the way east, nothing passes yet, dash and lunge included; a thin slit at x 40 jumped where the path crosses it, a short one at x 58), the stepping path (mt2, out of the rise's pass, a close view sliding with you, grey stone throughout: plates on its banks you hop up and jump off, faces you go round; the drop eats the middle wall to wall and a chain of seven islands, each a sure jump from the last, is the only way across; one more off the chain, a long jump, with carrots; the pass leads south), then the climb (climb3 to climb5, each screen to be remade on the main game as the mountain, docs/mountain-plan.md), the crags and the High Reaches above the old summit, spore travel between found mushrooms, Wick's cellar.
- Islands are at least 2 tiles across, every gap a sure running jump; each screen keeps its own perspective (no special
  camera over jumps). Smaller platforms only if Ross asks.
- Ravines (Ross, 1 Oct): bottomless, no floor or end in sight (an end runs off the screen's edge or lies under something);
  the walls you see are the far ones, leaning toward the middle of the view and sliding as you walk; grey stones set in
  the walls from a tile and a half under the lip, a quarter of each showing, with earth over the join; a faint river at
  the bottom of the biggest only; nothing stands inside one; no islands of ground inside one (islands come from their own
  generator, laid on purpose); no spider branches, no round pits for now. Nothing on a mountain screen is walled off
  with rows of stones: the screen's edges and the mountain itself hold.
