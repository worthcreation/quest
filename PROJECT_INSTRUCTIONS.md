Quest: a browser RPG (Zelda meets EarthBound) in a single index.html, hosted at https://worthcreation.github.io/quest/ from the repo worthcreation/quest (personal GitHub account). The repo holds everything: index.html, src/ (split parts), tests/, tools/, HANDOFF.md.

Start of every chat: git clone https://github.com/worthcreation/quest.git into /home/claude, read HANDOFF.md, and edit the parts in src/. Assemble with: cd src && cat head.html p1.js p2.js p3.js p4.js p6.js p7.js p8.js p9.js p10.js p11.js p12.js p13.js p14.js p5.js > ../index.html (p5 last). Current build number is in p7.js (const BUILD); bump it every build.

Delivery: one download per build, quest-bNN.zip, laid out like the repo root (index.html, src/, tests/, tools/, HANDOFF.md, PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md; no node_modules). Update HANDOFF.md in it every build. End each reply with the ship command:
cd ~/quest && unzip -o ~/Downloads/quest-bNN.zip && rm ~/Downloads/quest-bNN.zip && git add . && git commit -m "Build NN: ..." && git push
and a play link with a fresh 7-digit prime seed: ?seed=N (also ?arena&seed=N and ?puzzle&seed=N when relevant).

Ways of working: build features headlessly and test them with the node harness (tests/t*.js style: eval index.html with a fake canvas) before delivering; report what was tested, plainly, with numbers; render PNGs with tools/shot.js when visuals change. Keep arena and puzzle modes on the same code as the main game (they only add a hub, a kit and System items). Lay out anything jumpable in tiles at enterScene, not in screen fractions. No em dashes in replies; concise, candid, no AI-sounding filler.

Design rules to keep: gusts come in sets of three (two gentle, one strong), only the strong one carries you, and it lands on a ledge with a landing shadow from the apex; wind never pushes you in the air or on rock/ledges. Rocks are buried and must be knocked loose (stomp), then rocked and heaved; cracked stones and the gremlin burrow need a thrown rock; gates are boulders wedged on a cracked keystone, and mud swallows rocks that land in it (pound and pull them out); no pressure plates, logs or ropes. Say "rich soil", never "tilled". Text boxes are placed once and stay fixed; prompts are a half-strength ring + key label, tips live in the menu marquee. The quest HUD is a light see-through note that flares bold on a milestone, then fades back. Slots A S D F sit under the vigor bar, each with a lane (F act + blade, D throw, S use, A seeds; tap to use, hold A or S to pick from that lane's wheel): whatever is in a slot, that key uses it; empty slots draw nothing; new things auto-fill empty slots (weapons into F) and never overwrite. F is also the dynamic action. Hold R for the wheel (use now), R + slot key to set a slot. D backs out of menus, F selects. Garden patches are few and sit mostly by travel mushrooms, never packed side by side (diagonal or a tile apart); Pip's garden has two. Crafting: the field mat makes camp pieces, charms, a wooden sword (splinters and shatters), blade augmentations and food; big weapon enhancements happen at the workbench. Known recipes lay themselves out and jump to Combine; the mat hints when one ingredient short or when one more might do more. Held text stays while relevant to where you are, then fades. Waiting speech (needs F) is a solid, edged box with the key badge; free speech is a light italic bubble. The tutorial is free text with varied reminders, Pip walking to what helps. Speech is a sentence or two at a time in a bubble over whoever is speaking, never a bottom panel. Pip leads well ahead on one side of your path and never circles you; speech bubbles keep their place beside the speaker and never dodge about; most of Pip's hints fade on their own. Quest starts and completions share one region-themed banner; completions add a line on what was done. The vigor bar grows to 17 (as wide as the slot row), then each further 17 lays a darker fill over the same bar; the filled slots sit centred under it. Quest banners are themed by region (all current areas are the Vale) and animate smoothly. Item sparkles are sparse and faint. The pack only shows tabs with something in them, in labelled sections; a Status tab shows all levels, and System has a Testing screen to set levels. Wearables (armaments, charms, flair) are chosen in the pack's Wear tab and always visible on the hero. Reuse the one slot model (slotOptions/useEntry/setSlot) rather than new per-feature lists. Speech and quest alerts hold until F (or a tap); Pip walks to what Pip points out and waits there; Pip is named "Pip", never "they". Items rest on the ground and are picked up with F (only spores and wisps by touch); no button is drawn for them, they give off a faint, occasional sparkle, a little more often up close. Pounding can shake loose hidden finds anywhere. A gathering skill grows with pickups: F's reach and a faint selection ring grow with it, things begin drifting to you on their own at higher levels (rarer things need more), and at the top almost anything on screen comes to you; hidden spots show with a keen eye. Skills level quietly: no numbers, one flavour line per level in #c9a2ff. Quests tab: current objectives on top, folded log below, newest first.

Story so far: opens on the old jetty mid-conversation (free to wander, exits closed until Pip's three lines are read and Pip heads south), Pip waits by the garden, robin seed lesson (first drop guaranteed), plant two seeds (quest: Pip's garden), Pip leads to the camp spot (quest: Set up camp; lean-to already up), gather river stones / forest sticks / rabbit fluff with Pip saying what's still needed, craft glue then fire ring and workbench on the mat (2 slots, then 3), twilight scene by lantern light in the lean-to, forest map run at dusk, gremlins take Pip down a small weird hole, smash it open with a rock, sword at w3, hidden to the hilt in a stump, with a slow, bright reveal. Pip and the gremlins can be chased during the abduction; they hop clear every time.
